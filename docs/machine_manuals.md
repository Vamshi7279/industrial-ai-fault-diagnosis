# Industrial Plant Machine Operating & Maintenance SOP Manuals

## 1. Primary Exhaust Cooling Fan (FAN-01)
* **Manufacturer**: SKF & FläktGroup Industrial Systems
* **Acoustic Baseline**: Normal reconstruction loss MSE threshold is 12.22.
* **Component 1 - High-Speed Rotor Bearing Assembly**:
  - Operating Temperature Limit: 75°C.
  - Recommended Lubricant: Mobilith SHC 220 Synthetic Grease.
  - Re-lubrication Interval: 1,500 operating hours.
  - Diagnostic Evidence: Frequency spectral distortion in the 200-400 Hz band indicates inner race bearing cage wear.
  - Spare Part: SKF-6208-2RS Deep Groove Ball Bearing (#SKF-6208-2RS).
* **Component 2 - Aerodynamic Fan Impeller Blade**:
  - Vibration Tolerance: < 2.8 mm/s RMS.
  - Common Faults: Material build-up, dynamic imbalance, blade pitting.

## 2. Conveyor Drive Gearbox (GEARBOX-01)
* **Manufacturer**: Bosch Rexroth Drive Technology
* **Acoustic Baseline**: Normal reconstruction loss MSE threshold is 11.85.
* **Component 1 - Helical Pinion Gear Teeth Assembly**:
  - Oil Viscosity: ISO VG 220 Gear Oil.
  - Oil Replacement Interval: 2,000 operating hours.
  - Diagnostic Evidence: High acoustic loss spikes above 18.5 indicate micro-pitting and gear tooth chipping.
  - Spare Part: Helical Pinion Gear Set (#BR-GEAR-410).
* **Component 2 - Input Shaft Synthetic Oil Seal**:
  - Temperature Limit: 90°C.
  - Spare Part: Viton Double Lip Shaft Seal (#BR-SEAL-90).

## 3. Hydraulic Circulation Pump (PUMP-01)
* **Manufacturer**: Grundfos Industrial Pumps
* **Acoustic Baseline**: Normal reconstruction loss MSE threshold is 12.05.
* **Component 1 - Hydraulic Impeller Vane Assembly**:
  - Fluid Velocity: 4.5 m/s.
  - Diagnostic Evidence: High acoustic noise in 1000-2500 Hz range indicates localized flow cavitation and impeller vane tip erosion.
  - Spare Part: Stainless Steel Impeller Kit (#GF-IMP-88).
* **Component 2 - Mechanical Shaft Seal Assembly**:
  - Leakage Tolerance: Zero visible drops.
  - Spare Part: Silicon Carbide Mechanical Seal (#GF-SEAL-12).

## 4. High-Pressure Steam Control Valve (VALVE-01)
* **Manufacturer**: Emerson Fisher Controls
* **Acoustic Baseline**: Normal reconstruction loss MSE threshold is 12.50.
* **Component 1 - Hardened Valve Seat & Plunger Assembly**:
  - Maximum Steam Pressure: 40 Bar.
  - Diagnostic Evidence: Persistent high loss profile indicates Stellite valve seat erosion causing internal steam leakage bypass.
  - Spare Part: Hardened Valve Seat & Plunger Assembly (#VLV-SET-102).
* **Component 2 - Pneumatic Actuator Diaphragm**:
  - Air Supply Pressure: 4.5 Bar.
  - Spare Part: Reinforced Neoprene Diaphragm (#EM-DIAPH-05).
